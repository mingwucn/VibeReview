# focus: ai_validation@1.0.0

## Domain emphasis

Scientific robustness is what separates a demonstrated result from a plausible
one. In AI-assisted manufacturing research, the load-bearing elements of
robustness are evaluation design, baselines, leakage control, generalization
testing, and reproducibility. This emphasis applies to every process family and
AI function: a monitoring model, a parameter optimizer, and a digital twin are
all scientific claims that must be validated before they are believed.

Evaluation design comes first: what data the model is trained and tested on,
how splits are constructed, and whether the test set is genuinely unseen in
the ways that matter — across time, batches, machines, materials, and sites.
Baselines must be strong and appropriate: comparisons against weak or outdated
baselines inflate nearly every reported gain. Leakage is the silent failure
mode — splits that straddle correlated samples, features that encode the
label, preprocessing fitted on test data — and claims without leakage analysis
should be treated as unvalidated.

Generalization is the decisive question for manufacturing AI: whether a model
trained on one machine, material, or regime retains its performance on
another, and whether the study tested it. Reproducibility covers data and code
availability, hyperparameter disclosure, and whether reported results can be
regenerated. Positive patterns to look for: external validation on
independent data, ablations, uncertainty estimates, and published failure
analyses.

## Terminology pointers

- evaluation design, experimental protocol, train/validation/test split
- baseline, state of the art (SOTA), ablation study, ablation
- data leakage, label leakage, target leakage, cross-validation, grouped split
- generalization, transfer, domain shift, out-of-distribution (OOD), external validation
- reproducibility, replication, open data, open code, hyperparameters
- statistical significance, confidence intervals, uncertainty quantification
- false positive rate, false support, benchmark, leaderboard
- cross-machine validation, cross-material validation, cross-site validation
