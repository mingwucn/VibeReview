# focus: physics_informed_ai@1.0.0

## Domain emphasis

Physics-informed AI combines machine learning with physical knowledge —
conservation laws, governing equations, known constraints — to improve data
efficiency, extrapolation, and interpretability beyond what purely data-driven
models achieve. In precision manufacturing the motivation is concrete: process
data is scarce, experiments are expensive, and the underlying physics (thermal,
electrochemical, fluid, mechanical) is partially known. Hybrid modelling,
physics-informed learning, uncertainty quantification, inverse design, and
transfer are the recurring scientific elements.

What matters scientifically is whether the physical structure buys anything
measurable: better prediction under sparse data, credible extrapolation
outside the training regime, calibrated uncertainty, or physically meaningful
parameters. Physics-informed models can also do worse than well-tuned
empirical models when the embedded physics is misspecified, and honest
comparisons against strong non-physics baselines are the difference between a
demonstrated advance and a plausible story. Inverse-design claims — models
that propose parameters or geometries to hit a target outcome — must show
experimental or high-fidelity validation of the proposed designs, not only
model-internal consistency.

Transfer is the most consequential promise: physics-informed and hybrid models
are claimed to generalize across machines, materials, and scales because
physics is shared. Treat this as a claim to be tested, not assumed. Look for
studies that validate transfer explicitly, quantify the uncertainty of
extrapolated predictions, and report when the physics-informed advantage
disappears.

## Terminology pointers

- physics-informed machine learning (PIML), physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)
- hybrid modelling, physics-based data-driven model, grey-box / gray-box model
- governing equations, constraints, conservation laws, constitutive model
- uncertainty quantification (UQ), Bayesian neural networks, calibrated uncertainty
- inverse design, inverse problem, design optimization, surrogate model
- transfer learning, domain adaptation, extrapolation, out-of-distribution
- multi-fidelity modelling, simulation-informed learning, model order reduction
- active learning, Bayesian experimental design, optimal experimental design
