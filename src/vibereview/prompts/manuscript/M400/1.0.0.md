# M400 — Manuscript-Outline Derivation (M400@1.0.0)

## Purpose

Derive the manuscript outline from the research outline plus the synthesized
evidence state. The manuscript outline is where evidence, not plan, determines
structure: sections exist, merge, split, or disappear according to what the
evidence supports, and controversies or limitations become structural elements
when the evidence demands them.

## Procedure

1. Read the components appended after this body by the deterministic compiler:
   the research outline, the validated ClaimPackets, the rejected claims with
   their deciding evidence, the recorded contradictions, the qualifications
   and boundary conditions synthesis requires, the evidence density per
   research section, and the known limitations of the corpus and retrieval
   state. The manuscript outline must be derivable from these components alone.
2. Map each research outline section to its evidence state: how many
   ClaimPackets it holds, how dense and how independent the underlying evidence
   is, what contradictions and qualifications attach to it, and what claims were
   rejected from it. Evidence density, not the plan's ambition, drives what
   survives.
3. Decide the fate of each research section: carry it into the manuscript as a
   section, merge it with a neighboring section where their ClaimPackets and
   themes do not sustain separate sections, split it where the evidence
   supports two distinct stories, delete it where the evidence cannot support
   it, or reorder it where the evidence's logic runs differently from the
   plan's.
4. Convert where the evidence demands it: a section whose dominant content is a
   live contradiction becomes a controversy section; a section whose evidence
   is thin, dependent, or contested becomes a limitations section. Record every
   conversion and its motivating evidence.
5. Ensure every retained claim has a home: for each ClaimPacket, name the
   manuscript section that will carry it. For each rejected claim that was
   prominent in the research plan, decide whether the manuscript must mention
   the rejected direction and why it is excluded; gap claims stay disabled per
   protocol.
6. Place the required representation elements: every contradiction,
   qualification, and boundary condition that synthesis marked as required must
   be assigned to a manuscript section in this outline, not deferred to prose
   time.
7. Check the reader's spine: introduction, scope and definitions,
   evaluation framework, the substantive sections, the robustness and
   outlook material, and conclusions must all exist in an order a critical
   narrative can follow. The future-direction material remains outlook only.
8. Record every structural decision — merge, split, delete, reorder, convert —
   with the evidence that motivated it. A manuscript outline that cannot
   justify its structure from the compiled evidence is not acceptable.
9. Do not draft prose, do not phrase section content as findings, and do not
   name literature; this artifact fixes structure only.

## Output Contract

```text
MANUSCRIPT OUTLINE

Spine:
- Introduction: <purpose and claim coverage>
- Scope, definitions, evaluation framework: <purpose>
- <substantive sections in order, with purpose and claim coverage>
- Robustness / reproducibility: <purpose>
- Future direction (outlook only): <purpose>
- Conclusions: <purpose>

Per manuscript section:
- Section ID and title: <MS-nn title>
- Purpose: <one sentence>
- Authorized ClaimPackets: <packet IDs>
- Required contradictions represented: ...
- Required qualifications represented: ...
- Required boundary conditions represented: ...
- Rejected-claim notes (if any): <direction, why excluded>
- Figures/tables planned: <none | <descriptions>>

Structural decision log:
- <research section -> manuscript fate>: <merge/split/delete/reorder/convert> |
  motivating evidence: ...

Claim coverage check:
- Every ClaimPacket has a home: confirmed
- Every required representation element has a home: confirmed
```

- Every structural change from the research outline is logged with motivating
  evidence.
- Every ClaimPacket and every required contradiction, qualification, and
  boundary condition has an assigned home section.
- The future-direction section is marked outlook only.
- No prose, no findings phrasing, no literature names.
