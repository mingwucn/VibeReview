# M450 — Sentence Audit (M450@1.0.0)

## Purpose

Audit every substantive rendered sentence for entailment before it can enter
the canonical manuscript. A sentence passes only if its proposition fully
entails it — every scientific assertion the sentence makes is already present
in the proposition it renders — and its citation bindings are exactly those the
proposition authorizes. Rendering may polish language; this audit guarantees
the polish changed nothing scientific.

## Procedure

1. Read the components appended after this body by the deterministic compiler:
   the section contract, the audited propositions that passed, and the rendered
   section prose with its per-sentence proposition annotations and citation
   bindings. Audit every substantive sentence; skip none.
2. For each substantive sentence, perform an entailment assessment against the
   proposition it claims to render: does the proposition, together with its
   citation bindings, fully entail every scientific assertion the sentence
   makes? Check that the sentence introduces no new claim, no new mechanism,
   no new example, number, comparison, qualifier, or caveat that the
   proposition does not already contain.
3. Check the reverse direction for under-reach that matters: if the sentence
   silently weakens the proposition — dropping a required qualification,
   softening a required contradiction, or omitting the known uncertainty — the
   sentence fails, since the contract requires those elements to stand.
4. Check citation bindings: the sentence's citations must be exactly the
   bindings authorized for the proposition — none added, none dropped, none
   re-pointed at evidence the proposition does not support.
5. Check terminology and scope: the sentence must use the proposition's terms
   at the proposition's scope; a narrower-scope term is a silent widening and
   fails.
6. Check organizational sentences separately: a purely connective sentence
   (signposting, transition, cross-reference) that asserts nothing scientific
   is not subject to entailment assessment, but must be flagged as connective
   so its non-substantive status is recorded.
7. Assign each substantive sentence exactly one verdict: ENTAILED, or the
   applicable failure verdict used by the frozen contract (for example
   REJECT, UNCLEAR, UNSUPPORTED, OVERSTATED, PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED). For any
   non-ENTAILED verdict, quote the offending span and state exactly which
   entailment or binding requirement it violates.
8. Record that only ENTAILED sentences proceed to canonical manuscript
   inclusion; failed sentences are returned to rendering with the audit
   finding, never fixed silently at audit time.
9. Confirm no unrestricted rewriting happens after this audit: the accepted
   sentence set is final for scientific content.

## Output Contract

```text
SENTENCE AUDIT — <manuscript section ID and title>

Sentence S-nn (proposition: P-nn):
- Text: <rendered sentence>
- Entailment assessment: <does the proposition fully entail every assertion
  in this sentence?>
- Required-element preservation: <qualification/contradiction/uncertainty intact>
- Citation binding check: <exact bindings, none added or dropped>
- Scope and terminology check: <matches proposition>
- Verdict: ENTAILED | REJECT | UNCLEAR | UNSUPPORTED | OVERSTATED | PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED
- If not ENTAILED: offending span: <quoted text>; violated requirement: <which>

<repeat per substantive sentence>

Connective sentences recorded: <list, marked non-substantive>

Acceptance summary:
- ENTAILED sentences proceeding to canonical manuscript inclusion: <list>
- Failed sentences returned to rendering with findings: <list>

FINALITY STATEMENT:
Only ENTAILED sentences proceed to canonical manuscript inclusion. Accepted
sentences are final for scientific content; no unrestricted rewriting occurs
after this audit.
```

- Every substantive sentence receives an explicit entailment assessment
  against its proposition and citation bindings.
- Verdicts are drawn from the frozen contract's sentence-audit verdict set.
- Only ENTAILED sentences are listed as proceeding to canonical manuscript
  inclusion.
