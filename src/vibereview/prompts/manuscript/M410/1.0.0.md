# M410 — Manuscript Section Contract (M410@1.0.0)

## Purpose

Write the contract for one manuscript section. The section contract is the
binding authorization for everything the section may say: it names the
ClaimPackets the section may use, the contradictions and qualifications it must
represent, the assertions it must not make, and the figures and tables it may
include. No prose generation is permitted for a section without its contract.

## Procedure

1. Read the components appended after this body by the deterministic compiler:
   the manuscript outline (this section's purpose, claim coverage, and planned
   figures/tables), the full ClaimPackets authorized to the section, the
   synthesis records behind them (outcomes, certainty levels, required
   representations), and the known limitations relevant to the section.
2. Write the section's purpose as one sentence a reader could use to predict
   what the section argues. The purpose must be narrower than the ClaimPackets
   allow, never broader.
3. List the authorized ClaimPackets exactly: every packet the section may draw
   on, by ID, with its certainty level. No prose stage may cite evidence
   outside this list for this section.
4. State the required contradiction: the contradiction(s) synthesis marked as
   required for this section's claims and that the section must present
   honestly, not bury.
5. State the required qualification: the qualification(s) the section must
   carry in its own voice — scope limits, conditionality, dependence — drawn
   from synthesis, not invented here.
6. State the known uncertainty: what remains genuinely unsettled about the
   section's topic at the evidence's strongest honest reading, phrased so prose
   cannot glide past it.
7. Write the prohibited assertions: concrete statement shapes the section must
   not make — stronger causality than the packets support, broader scope than
   the packets' boundary conditions, certainty above the packets' level,
   transfer claims the evidence does not test, and any gap claim disabled by
   protocol. Phrase prohibitions as checkable statement patterns, not vibes.
8. Specify the figures and tables the section may include, each tied to
   authorized ClaimPackets, with the exact evidence it may present. A figure
   may not show claims the section is not authorized to make.
9. Check the contract against the manuscript outline: coverage matches, nothing
   unauthorized enters, every required representation element from synthesis is
   present. Check the contract is enforceable: every item must be checkable by
   the proposition and sentence audits.
10. If the section cannot be contracted honestly — its purpose exceeds its
    packets, or its required representations cannot be carried — say so and
    mark the section for outline revision instead of writing a contract that
    overpromises.

## Output Contract

```text
SECTION CONTRACT — <manuscript section ID and title>

Purpose: <one sentence>

Authorized ClaimPackets:
- <packet ID>: <one-line content summary> | certainty: <level>

Required contradiction:
- <the contradiction this section must present>

Required qualification:
- <the qualification this section must carry>

Known uncertainty:
- <what remains unsettled>

Prohibited assertions:
- <checkable statement pattern the section must not make>
- ...

Figures/tables:
- <figure/table ID>: <what it shows> | bound to packets: <IDs>
  (or: none)

Enforceability check:
- Coverage matches manuscript outline: confirmed
- No unauthorized evidence: confirmed
- All required representations present: confirmed
- Audit-checkable: confirmed

Outline-revision flag (if the section cannot be contracted honestly):
- <what fails and what outline change is needed>
```

- No prose generation may occur for this section without this contract.
- Every authorized packet is listed by ID with its certainty level.
- Prohibited assertions are checkable statement patterns.
- A figure or table may only present claims the section is authorized to make.
