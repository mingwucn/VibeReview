# P040 — Outline Revision Proposal (P040@1.0.0)

## Purpose

Turn the human-approved outline challenge decisions into a concrete revision
proposal that produces the next version of the research outline. The revision
proposal is the audit trail between outline versions: it records exactly what
changed, why, and who authorized the change.

## Procedure

1. Read the components appended after this body by the deterministic compiler:
   the previous research outline version, the challenge decision list with the
   human's per-item decisions, and the project brief for scope-boundary checking.
2. Adopt only decisions the human explicitly approved. If a challenge item was
   marked UNRESOLVED or its human decision is missing, record it as carried over
   unchanged; never resolve it yourself.
3. For every approved decision, translate it into concrete outline edits. Apply
   edits in an order that keeps the outline internally consistent: renames before
   merges, merges before moves, moves before renumbering; additions and removals
   before the final renumbering pass.
4. Renumber the outline after all structural edits so that top-level sections are
   gap-free S01...Snn and decimal subsections are gap-free under each parent. No
   ordinal may be skipped within a level.
5. Record every change as a before/after pair: the item exactly as it appeared in
   the previous version, the item exactly as it now appears, and the challenge
   decision (item ID and verdict) that authorized the change.
6. Verify the proposed new version: every section and subsection still carries a
   scope statement and an expected-content statement; every change traces to an
   approved decision; no content was dropped silently; scope boundaries and
   non-goals of the project brief are still respected.
7. Propose the new version under the next unused version ordinal. Never overwrite,
   rename, or edit the previous version; the proposal references it and stands
   beside it.
8. Do not draft prose, do not name literature, and do not smuggle in structural
   changes that no approved decision authorizes.

## Output Contract

```text
OUTLINE REVISION PROPOSAL

Source version: <previous outline version ID>
Proposed new version: <next version ID>
Previous version status: unchanged, retained as-is

Change log (one entry per change):
1. Decision: <challenge item ID + verdict>
   Before: <exact prior item text or "absent">
   After: <exact new item text or "removed">
2. ...

Carried-over unresolved items:
- <item ID>: <UNRESOLVED or missing decision; unchanged>

Renumbering map (if any):
- <old ID> -> <new ID>

Verification:
- Every change traces to an approved decision: confirmed
- Scope and expected content present for all items: confirmed
- No silently dropped content: confirmed
- Project-brief boundaries and non-goals respected: confirmed

Proposed outline (full text of the new version):
<complete numbered outline S01...Snn with subsections>
```

- One change-log entry per change, each with before text, after text, and the
  authorizing decision.
- The previous version is explicitly recorded as retained unchanged.
- Every item in the proposed outline has a scope statement and an expected-content
  statement.
- No literature, citations, or drafted prose may appear.
