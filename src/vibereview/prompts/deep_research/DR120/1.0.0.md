# DR120 — Adversarial Review Deep Research (DR120@1.0.0)

## Purpose

Run an adversarial Deep Research pass whose goal is to break the emerging review
narrative, not to confirm it. The run is executed after a first draft of the
review exists or is emerging, and it hunts for the evidence most likely to
invalidate the review's claims, structure, or confidence. A successful run
surfaces material that forces revision; a run that finds nothing must say so
explicitly and list what it checked.

## Procedure

1. Read the components appended after this body by the deterministic compiler:
   the project brief, the protocol, the current outline or draft narrative being
   attacked, the focus modules in declared order, and any supplement. Your target
   is the review's emerging narrative as stated in those components.
2. Adopt the adversarial stance: assume the narrative is wrong somewhere and your
   task is to find where. Do not summarize the field neutrally; search for what
   undermines the review.
3. Seek contradictory studies: research that reports opposite findings to the
   claims the review relies on. For each, give a full bibliographic reference,
   state the exact point of contradiction, and assess whether it is a direct
   contradiction of the claim or a contradiction of its scope conditions.
4. Seek null results: studies that expected but did not find an effect, with
   enough methodological detail to judge whether the null is informative.
5. Seek alternative mechanisms: different causal accounts of effects the review
   attributes to one mechanism. Record each mechanism and the evidence for it.
6. Seek methodological objections: flaws in the studies the review relies on —
   evaluation design, baselines, leakage, dataset scale and diversity, metric
   choice, missing ablations, reproducibility gaps.
7. Seek missing materials and populations: materials, machines, geometries,
   datasets, process regimes, or application domains the review generalizes to
   but the evidence does not cover.
8. Seek missed landmark papers: well-known, highly cited, or field-defining work
   the review's corpus and narrative omit. Check whether each omission is
   material.
9. Seek recent challenges: the most recent work contesting the review's claims,
   including preprints where identifiable, and state how each challenge is backed.
10. For every finding, state its target: the specific claim, section, or
    structural element of the review it threatens, and the severity of the threat
    (narrative-breaking, scope-limiting, or minor).
11. If a category yields nothing, record that explicitly with the search terms
    and sources checked, so the absence of evidence is itself auditable.
12. Close by stating explicitly that this output is discovery material, not
    canonical evidence, and that any relevant literature re-enters the review
    only through the authorized evidence pipeline (new corpus pin, then rerun of
    affected claims) — never by patching the manuscript directly.

Execution boundary: a human operator executes this compiled prompt manually —
copying the exact compiled text into the provider interface without editing,
running the research, and saving the raw report unchanged for VibeReview
registration, which binds the report bytes to this prompt's recorded hash.
No automated execution is part of this procedure.

## Output Contract

```text
ADVERSARIAL REVIEW REPORT

Target narrative: <the claims/structure under attack>

Contradictory studies:
- <full reference> | contradicts: <specific claim> | direct or scope-conditional | severity: ...
- ...

Null results:
- <full reference> | expected effect absent | methodological detail: ...
- ...

Alternative mechanisms:
- <mechanism> | evidence: ... | threatens: <claim or section>
- ...

Methodological objections:
- <objection> | affects studies: ... | severity: ...
- ...

Missing materials and populations:
- <what the review generalizes to without evidence>
- ...

Missed landmark papers:
- <full reference> | omission material: yes/no, why
- ...

Recent challenges:
- <full reference or preprint> | challenge backed by: ... | severity: ...
- ...

Checked-with-no-result categories:
- <category> | search terms and sources checked
- ...

DISCOVERY-ONLY STATEMENT:
This report is discovery material, not canonical evidence. Any relevant
literature re-enters only through the authorized evidence pipeline; the
manuscript is never patched directly.
```

- Every category above must be addressed; empty categories must record what was
  checked.
- Every finding names the review element it targets and a severity.
- No finding may be softened, ranked up, or omitted to protect the narrative.
